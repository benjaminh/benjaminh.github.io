During my PhD, I worked as a project manager for [Nantes History Museum](https://www.chateaunantes.fr). The project aimed at promoting a 1899 scale model representing Nantes harbour. From 2011 to 2015, we worked on the knowledge database system, and the design of a dedicated augmented reality setup. The final setup is still visible within the Museum tour.
